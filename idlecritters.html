Privacy Policy

Idle Critter Collectors · Effective date: September 17, 2026 · Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Saba Roriaki ("we", "us", or "developer") handles information in the game Idle Critter Collectors (the "Game"). We built the Game to respect your privacy and to collect as little information as possible.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information. The Game does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, or other personal details to play.

Game progress. Your in-game progress (such as coins, upgrades, critters, quests, and settings) is saved locally on your device or browser so you can continue where you left off. This data stays on your device and is not collected by us.

When you play the Game on CrazyGames.com, the CrazyGames platform may collect and process certain information (such as device and usage data, and advertising or account features it provides) in accordance with its own policies. Please review the CrazyGames Privacy Policy for details on how CrazyGames handles your data on its platform.

3. Advertising

If advertisements are shown, they are delivered through the CrazyGames platform and its advertising partners, not by us. Any data used for advertising is handled by CrazyGames and its partners under their policies.

4. Children's Privacy

The Game is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe any personal information has been provided to us, please contact us and we will address it.

5. Data Security and Retention

Because game progress is stored locally on your device, you remain in control of it. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser or app data. We do not maintain a personal database of players.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding personal data under laws such as the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA. Since we do not collect personal information directly, most requests relating to platform data should be directed to CrazyGames. You may also contact us using the details below with any privacy questions.
