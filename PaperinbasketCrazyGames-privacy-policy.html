2. Information We Collect

We designed the Game to collect as little data as possible. The Game itself does not require you to create an account, and it does not ask for your real name, email address, phone number, or payment details.

a) Information stored on your own device

The Game stores the following locally in your browser (using your browser's storage, e.g. local storage / IndexedDB):

  • A display name you optionally type in for the leaderboard.
  • Your scores and local high-score/leaderboard entries.
  • Settings such as whether audio is muted.

This information stays on your device. It is used only to run the Game (for example, to show your local leaderboard) and is not transmitted to us as a personal profile. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

b) Information collected by the gaming platform and advertising

The Game uses the CrazyGames SDK to display advertisements and to report basic gameplay events (such as when a round starts and ends). Through this SDK and the platform it runs on, the following may be processed by CrazyGames and its advertising partners:

  • Technical and device information (for example, browser type, device type, approximate region, and IP address).
  • Advertising identifiers and cookies or similar technologies used to serve and measure ads.
  • Aggregated gameplay and engagement metrics (for example, play sessions and retention).

We do not control how CrazyGames and its advertising partners collect or use this data. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies.

The Game does not use rewarded ads and does not offer in-game purchases.

3. How We Use Information

We use the limited information described above to:

  • Run the Game and remember your local settings, name, and scores.

  • Show advertising through the CrazyGames SDK so the Game can remain free to play.
  • Understand, in aggregate, how the Game is performing (for example, how long people play).
