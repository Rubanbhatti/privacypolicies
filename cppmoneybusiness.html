Privacy Policy

CPP Money Business · Effective date: September 14, 2026 · Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Saba Roriaki ("we", "us", or "developer") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the mobile game CPP Money Business (the "Game"). By creating an account or using the Game, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account or sign in, we collect the email address you provide and an authentication credential (password), which is handled securely by our authentication provider. We do not store your password ourselves.

Game progress data. To save your progress and let you continue on other devices, we store your in‑game data — such as coins, upgrades, characters, quests, prestige progress, and settings — associated with a unique account identifier (user ID).

Automatically processed data. Our backend provider (Google Firebase) may process limited technical information, such as your device's IP address and basic device/usage data, as part of delivering authentication and database services.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and sign you in.
  • To save, load, and synchronize your game progress across sessions and devices.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Game.
  • To protect against fraud, abuse, and security issues.

We do not sell your personal information. The Game does not currently show third‑party advertising or use third‑party analytics tracking.

3. Third‑Party Services

The Game uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database) to provide accounts and cloud saving. Your information is processed by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase Privacy and Security terms.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your account and game data are stored on Google Firebase servers. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account and game data for as long as your account exists. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below, or by using the in‑app account deletion option where available. Upon a verified request, we will delete your account information and game data, except where retention is required for legal or security reasons.

6. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR or the California CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. International Data Transfers

Your information may be stored and processed on servers located outside your country of residence, including in regions where Google Firebase operates. Where required, appropriate safeguards are applied to such transfers.
