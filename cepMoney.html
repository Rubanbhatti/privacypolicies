Privacy Policy

CepMoney · Effective date: August 20, 2026
The short version: CepMoney does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your own device. We have no servers, no accounts, and no analytics.

1. Who we are

CepMoney ("the App") is developed and published by Dian Gorjomeladze ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

Contact: diangorjom@icloud.com

2. Information you enter into the App

The App lets you record wellness information such as:

  • Name or nickname, age, sex, height, and weight
  • Fitness goals and activity level
  • Weight, water, exercise, sleep, and habit records
  • Personal goals and app settings

All of this information is stored locally on your device only. It is never uploaded, synced, or transmitted to us or to anyone else. We cannot see, access, or recover it.

3. Information we collect automatically

None. The App does not use analytics services, tracking technologies, advertising identifiers, cookies for tracking, or crash-reporting tools that send data off your device. The App requires no account and no internet connection for its core features.

4. Data sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your data with any third party — because your data never reaches us in the first place.

If you choose to use the "Export My Data" feature, the App creates a file on your device. What you do with that file (saving it, sending it, sharing it) is entirely under your control and happens through your device's standard sharing features.

5. Notifications

If you enable reminders, they are generated locally on your device (for example, water or workout reminders). No server is involved, and enabling notifications does not send any information to us. You can turn them off at any time in the App or in your device settings.

6. Health data and device integrations

If a future version of the App offers optional integration with your device's health platform (such as Apple HealthKit), it will only read or write data with your explicit permission, use it solely to display your own information inside the App, and never transmit it off your device. We will never use health data for advertising or share it with third parties.

7. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device for as long as you keep it. You can permanently erase everything at any time using Settings → Reset all data inside the App, or by deleting the App from your device.

8. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children — because it collects no personal information at all.

9. Not medical advice

CepMoney is intended for general wellness and informational purposes only. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any disease and is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always consult a qualified health professional with questions about your health.

10. Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the App handles data, we will update this policy and revise the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
